About Me

My name is Loukas Vasilakis. I grew up in Greece and moved to Switzerland in 2014. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Data Science at FHNW. Before beginning my studies, I completed an apprenticeship as an optician in Basel and subsequently worked in the field. My professional experience taught me how to understand individual needs, analyze different situations, and offer tailored solutions. Over time, I discovered that I particularly enjoyed the analytical aspects of my work. This interest in understanding complex problems and finding effective solutions ultimately motivated me to pursue a degree in Data Science. Beyond data science and software development, I enjoy traveling and literature.
