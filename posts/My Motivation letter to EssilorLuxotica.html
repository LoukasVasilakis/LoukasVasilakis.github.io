Motivation letter

Application for the Data Scientist Position

Basel October 15, 2026

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the Data Scientist position at EssilorLuxottica. It would be an honor to work for a company formed by the union of two industry leaders that have shaped the world of optics: Essilor, known for its innovations in lens technology, particularly the renowned Varilux progressive lenses, and Luxottica, whose eyewear portfolio includes high-performance sports and lifestyle eyewear from Oakley, fashionable frames from Dolce & Gabbana, and the iconic sunglasses of Ray-Ban.

As an optician, I have gained extensive knowledge of both lenses and frames. Through my professional experience, I have learned not only how to explain a product's advantages but also how to guide customers toward solutions that genuinely improve their daily lives. I have worked with customers with complex visual needs who required specific types of progressive lenses, as well as customers who needed carefully selected frame materials to avoid allergic reactions. Beyond technical expertise, I have developed the ability to communicate with customers of all ages, from young first-time glasses wearers to those with decades of experience. These interactions have taught me how to assess individual needs and identify the most suitable solutions.

My passion for analyzing individual situations and finding the best possible solutions motivated me to pursue a degree in Data Science. Throughout my studies, I have gained practical experience in machine learning through Python projects, including the development of predictive models and recommendation algorithms. These projects have strengthened my ability to translate theoretical concepts into practical solutions. Combined with my professional experience in optics, I believe this technical foundation makes me a strong candidate for the Data Scientist position at EssilorLuxottica. I understand the importance of domain knowledge when translating ambiguous business challenges into clearly defined data science problems, and I am particularly eager to research, experiment with, and develop innovative AI techniques that can be applied to real-world challenges.

I am eager to apply my analytical and technical skills to help develop innovative solutions for the eyewear industry. The opportunity to return to the optical industry and contribute to its development through data science would be particularly meaningful to me. Being able to combine my passion for technology with my professional background in optics is exactly the kind of opportunity I am looking for.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss in an interview how my professional background, technical skills, and motivation could contribute to your team. Thank you for considering my application.
